# Built around real work

Compai grew from the tools and memory system built inside LAAGAM, with operators and engineers working together.

The work includes store and product views, invoice preparation, campaign analysis, delivery tracking and private team workflows. They share context and controlled connections.

The [project collection](https://usecompai.com/projects.html) shows illustrative interfaces and reusable methods, not independently installable copies of private applications.

Business figures, costs, employee records and runtime telemetry are not published. Demos use independently fictional records and no live company connection.
