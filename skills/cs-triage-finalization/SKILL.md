# Close a customer care case

Use after a care draft or escalation exists. Read the original ticket, current order and approved policy. Identify what remains: a factual check, an owner decision, an authorised source action or a customer reply. Escalate payment, duties, refund and carrier exceptions to the assigned owner. Verify any authorised action in its source system and read back the ticket state. Close only when the required action and response are complete. Record unresolved cases explicitly. Do not claim a baseline resolution rate or send external messages without authority.

## Verification

Test with fictional records, a missing source and an unauthorised action. Keep the evidence, failures and human decision separate.
