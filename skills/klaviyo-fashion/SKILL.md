# Lifecycle marketing review

Use when reviewing lifecycle messages or segments. Retrieve the approved brand voice and current consent rules. Read campaign and flow performance from the email platform, and reconcile revenue definitions with the commerce source. Compare compatible cohorts and periods. Draft a change with a hypothesis, audience, exclusion rules and measurement plan. A human approves copy, incentive, recipient scope and send. Do not infer performance benchmarks or prescribe a discount from a generic template. Verify suppression and unsubscribe handling before activation.

## Verification

Test with fictional records, a missing source and an unauthorised action. Keep the evidence, failures and human decision separate.
