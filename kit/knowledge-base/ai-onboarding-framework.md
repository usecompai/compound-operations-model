# Team onboarding

Authenticate each person with their own identity. Show them the context and capabilities available to their role, then complete a useful job together.

Start with a fictional record. Find its sources, prepare a result, check the approval boundary and verify the output. Explain where the result is stored and how to correct it.

Never share credentials or use a colleague's session. Missing access should be repaired by the capability owner, not by sharing tokens. Keep personal documents private and use approved integrations.

Review actual completed work and the difficulties people report. This template makes no adoption or productivity claim.
