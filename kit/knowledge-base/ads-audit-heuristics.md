# Paid-media review checklist

Use company-approved thresholds and attribution definitions. This template does not prescribe spend allocations, performance targets or platform benchmarks.

Check consent and tracking, event deduplication, attribution windows, campaign objectives, creative coverage and audience exclusions. Compare spend with reconciled sales and contribution, not platform revenue alone.

Separate observations from hypotheses. Account for seasonality, stock, promotions and delayed returns. Prepare a bounded proposal with its owner, evidence, measurement window and stop condition.

Do not apply budget or audience changes without the authorised approval. Verify any approved write in the platform and retain rollback evidence.
