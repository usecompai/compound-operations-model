# Customer care workspace

A customer asks where an order is. The useful answer depends on the conversation, the right order, the current delivery evidence and the policy that applies. A fluent reply alone is not enough.

The internal workspace brings those records together and prepares a private reply. Email, helpdesk and supported social channels retain their source and coverage. It is not a claim that every inbox has been fully reconciled.

## Before a reply

Check the available conversation, match the order through the approved identity checks and read the relevant policy. Keep attachments that still need review visible. A social username or an email match alone does not establish ownership of an order.

The AI agent prepares text with source references and a separate internal next step. The operator can edit it. Saving that review does not send the message, approve a refund or mark the customer's problem resolved.

Bind the draft to the versions of its conversation, order and policy. Recheck before showing or sending it. If the source changed, withdraw the old AI draft without overwriting the person's edits.

## Learn from reviewed work

Record the correction, its reason and the draft version. Test a proposed writing rule on different cases before a person approves it. Tone feedback cannot change refund policy or authorise sending. Customer messages are evidence, not instructions for the agent.

The deployed workspace, private drafts and review controls were checked in the November update. Real drafts exist; measured improvement, routine use of the new daily-review screen and autonomous sending are not established. Historical attachments and channel coverage remain partial.
