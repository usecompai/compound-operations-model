# Receivables workspace

An operator-run workflow brings accounting documents, bank movements and authorised customer email into a shared receivables register. Collection, growth and follow-up views are published from the reviewed result.

## What happens

Match a receipt to a document using evidence, not just a similar customer name. Carry an advance into its invoice without treating it as a second payment. Keep unmatched receipts separate. A promise in an email is context, not bank evidence.

The record preserves correspondence, payments, unresolved matches and collection holds. The team can prepare follow-up text; sending remains manual. A new snapshot does not establish continuous synchronisation, and email coverage may be partial.

The operator's recent source checks, write-backs and publication were reviewed on 14 October 2026. The deployed collection, growth and follow-up pages match the recorded hashes from that run.

[Explore the receivables workspace](https://usecompai.com/projects/receivables-workspace.html). All public accounts and records are fictional. No company balance, bank identifier or customer conversation is published.

## Applying the method elsewhere

The later forecast update uses the same invoice eligibility rules across collection and growth views. Only eligible outstanding amounts enter the forecast. A verified payment promise can change an expected date; it cannot add the same amount a second time. Partial payments reduce what remains, and disputed or held items stay separate. This is a forecast of expected receipts, not the [cash position](31-cash-position.md).

Separate document currency from reporting currency. Preserve original receipts and existing notes. Check for changed source records before writing. Read back every intended change and block follow-up when a payment match is unresolved.
