# Evaluate your own deployment

Compai does not publish company costs, performance, adoption or operational volumes. Results from one business are not a forecast for another.

Choose a specific job and define completion. Measure current effort, waiting time, error handling and review. Document what is included before comparing alternatives.

Include source subscriptions, model usage, compute, storage, integration, monitoring and maintenance. A model bill alone is not total cost.

Compare completed outcomes on a reviewed sample. Separate saved effort from effort shifted into checking. Include rejected outputs, incidents and rollback work. Generated tasks are not value delivered.

Use your own evidence and assumptions. Keep the assessment private and update it as the workflow changes. Reference templates do not guarantee a return.
