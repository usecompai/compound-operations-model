# Truth and evidence

Establish which source is authoritative for the fact being checked. Read the owning system for current operational facts. Use the dated canonical record for a policy. Use the original meeting, email or chat for intent. An inference is not a verified fact.

```yaml
claim: "The fictional invoice draft exists"
source: "example-accounting-fixture"
state: verified
checked_at: "<actual verification time>"
owner: "<responsible role>"
audience: "<permitted audience>"
expires_at: "<review deadline>"
```

Distinguish a shipped pattern from a deployed capability, and availability from a completed outcome. A green health check does not prove a business action happened.

Compai does not publish company telemetry. Public examples are independently fictional. Keep a private evidence register for your deployment, with reproducible checks and review dates.
