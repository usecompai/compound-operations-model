# Working notes: November 2026

This update covers changes reviewed between 18 October and 1 November. It adds methods and examples, not a public copy of the company's applications.

## What changed

| Area | Added or improved | Where it stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Customer care](04-agent-cs.md) | Private reply drafts tied to the conversation, order and reviewed policy; feedback and source-change checks. | People review and send. No measured improvement or autonomous sending is claimed. |
| [Shipment tracking](34-shipment-tracking.md) | Carrier events attached to an exact order, with parcel direction and timestamps. | Coverage varies. A missing event is not a delivery status. |
| [People portal](09c-agent-hr.md) | Requests, personal records and reviewed departures linked to scheduled removal of Brain access while preserving work. | Leave-calculation differences remain. Departure handling does not revoke every external software account. |
| [Hiring](30-hiring.md) | The path from an application and signed offer to reviewed documents, approved activation and assigned onboarding tasks. | The new handoff passed fictional end-to-end checks; a completed real hire has not been verified through it. |
| [Creative work](35-creative-work.md) | Reproducible video edits, captions, exports and technical checks; reporting can inform the next brief. | People approve the edit. No automatic publishing or performance lift is claimed. |
| Finance and product reporting | Shared rules for outstanding invoices and payment promises; product reports preserve source dates and versions. | A promise is not cash. Refreshing a page does not refresh its source. |
| Retail planning | Shared proposals retain their shipment plan and revision history; conflicting edits are rejected rather than overwritten. | The interface is published; real web acceptance by a second account remains pending. It makes no stock movements. |
| [Checked execution](36-checked-work.md) | Source-bound drafts, bounded attempts, restricted service users and checks from a chat request through delivery of the answer. | An uncertain write must be reconciled before it is repeated. Delivery acceptance is not proof of reading. |

## Code in this release

The starter kit adds [small, tested workflow checks](../kit/patterns/checked-work/README.md). They can be inspected and adapted without connecting to company systems. The source index, project examples and downloads use the same release.

## Not included

Proposed invoice-review changes and connectors without sufficient evidence are not presented as working additions. Customer messages, employee documents, company figures and internal access details remain private.
