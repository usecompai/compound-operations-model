# Compai, from inside LAAGAM

Compai shares the company-memory architecture, tools and working methods developed around LAAGAM's operations. It brings together what the team knows and the systems where the work happens.

The public material is a reference to inspect and adapt, not a hosted copy of the company or a turnkey replacement for its systems. Private applications, people records and business figures are not included.

Start with one job. Name its source systems, the person responsible and the result that would make it complete. Connect only what that job needs. Test the whole path before adding more agents.

Read [the architecture](03-architecture.md), explore [the projects](https://usecompai.com/projects.html), or inspect [the starter kit](../kit/README.md).
