# Ads control tower

The internal workspace brings paid-media feeds into a shared reporting period. Channel comparisons, campaign and creative views sit alongside source freshness and attribution coverage. The team uses it in its paid-media review.

## Keep the views separate

Platform-reported sales describe that platform's attribution. Linked analytics may use a different definition. Neither is a company accounting result.

A separate economic view combines commerce, returns and costs for the selected period. It remains an estimate with coverage and certification gaps. Monthly accounting follows its own reconciliation process. Company-level economics cannot be relabelled as the profit caused by an individual campaign.

## What was checked

The overview and its date filters were tested after repairing a memory failure in weekly history. Source reconciliation, attribution labels and the read-only boundary remain part of the checks. Recent operator use is documented privately. The public review is dated 14 October 2026, not continuous telemetry.

[Explore the ads control tower](https://usecompai.com/projects/ads-control-tower.html). Its preview uses independently fictional channels and states, with no company figures or account identifiers. The earlier Performance pulse illustration remains withdrawn.

## Applying the method elsewhere

Match reporting windows and currencies. Preserve each source's attribution definition. Missing coverage is not zero spend. Keep estimates separate from accounting and require the authorised person to decide budget changes. This tool does not mutate advertising campaigns or prove incremental lift.
